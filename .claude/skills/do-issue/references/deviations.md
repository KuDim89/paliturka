# Deviations from the plan

The approved plan is followed as written. No silent improvising.

## Significant: stop

Stop before writing the different code:

- the approach doesn't work or must change;
- a step is added, dropped or replaced;
- files or areas outside the plan;
- a new dependency;
- an acceptance criterion can't be met as planned, or would be checked differently;
- an open decision from `CLAUDE.md` turns up.

Unsure whether it is significant → treat it as significant.

## Minor: no stop

Names, small helpers, step order within the same approach, typos. List them in the report under "Small deviations".

## On stop

1. Say what was found and why the plan doesn't fit, with evidence. Mark guesses as guesses.
2. Say what is already done.
3. Give the options.
4. Plan gate with the revised plan; continue from the changed step after approval.

A deviation re-plan is not a fix cycle and doesn't count toward the 3-cycle ceiling.
